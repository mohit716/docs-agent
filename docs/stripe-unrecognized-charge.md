# A charge you do not recognize

This note is for a cardholder who sees a Stripe charge on a bank statement and does not know who charged them. It is not a refund, a dispute, a payout, or a payment method setting.

## Who charged the card

Stripe did not sell the item. A business used Stripe to take the payment. The statement can show Stripe next to that business.

## What to check

Read the statement descriptor on the charge. It often includes the business name. Also search the cardholder email for a receipt from the same day and amount.

## Next step

Contact that business about the charge. The Stripe Dashboard is for the merchant, not for the cardholder who received the statement.
