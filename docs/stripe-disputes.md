# Challenging a dispute

This note is the support procedure for a card dispute on a Stripe charge. A dispute is not a refund. The cardholder starts it with their card issuer.

## Choice

The merchant can accept the dispute or challenge it. Accepting it returns the charge. Challenging it asks the card network to review evidence.

## Evidence

Submit proof that the customer received the goods or the service, the receipt, and messages with the customer. The deadline is shown on the dispute in the Dashboard. If no response is sent by that deadline, the dispute is lost.

## Dashboard

Open the dispute from the Disputes list. Choose to challenge it, attach the evidence, and submit the response.

## Outcome

The card network decides the dispute. The merchant does not decide it from the Dashboard.
