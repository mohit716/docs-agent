# Receiving a payout

This note is the support procedure for a Stripe payout. A payout sends the merchant's available balance to a bank account. It is not a refund and it is not a dispute.

## Bank account

A payout requires a bank account linked to the Stripe account. Add that account from the Dashboard before expecting money to arrive.

## First payout

The first payout waits 7 to 14 days. The wait is a risk check on the new account. Later payouts follow the schedule shown in the Dashboard.

## Late or missing

Compare the arrival date with the expected delivery date on the payout. Then check the Dashboard and the account email. Those two places show a delay, a failed bank account, or a payout that was not sent.
